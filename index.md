## 🏗️ Example Project: Planning a Bathroom Renovation

Let's walk through a typical scenario to show you how the tool works in practice.

**1. Create a New Project**
Open the app and click "New Project." Name it "Master Bathroom Remodel." Choose a category (e.g., "Bathroom Renovation") and set a target completion date.

**2. Build Your Material List**
Navigate to the "Materials" tab. Start adding items like:
- Ceramic tiles (30 square feet, $3.50/sq ft)
- Vanity cabinet ($250)
- New toilet ($180)
- Paint (2 gallons, $30 each)
- Grout, adhesive, sealant ($45 total)

**3. Compare Costs**
For each material, you can add multiple supplier prices. For the tiles, enter prices from two different home improvement stores. The app will show you the cheapest option and calculate the difference.

**4. Take Notes**
Go to the "Notes" tab. Write down:
- "Tile size: 12x24 inches, color: Alpine White"
- "Measurements: Floor is 8ft x 5ft, walls up to 6ft high"
- "Get extra grout—the gap around the tub is wider than expected"

**5. Track Time**
Use the "Time Tracking" tab to record when you start and finish each task. For example, start the "Remove old vanity" task on Monday morning and stop it when done. The app will show the total time spent.

**6. Review and Adjust**
Check your "Budget Overview" to see the total estimated cost. If it's over your budget, look for materials with high costs and find alternatives using the cost comparison feature.
